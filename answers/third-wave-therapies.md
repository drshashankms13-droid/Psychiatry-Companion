## Exam answer

### Concept

Third-wave cognitive-behavioural therapies emphasize context, acceptance, mindfulness, values, emotional regulation, and the function of thoughts, in addition to changing thought content. The term is a historical grouping, not a single uniform model.

### Major therapies

1. **ACT:** Builds psychological flexibility through acceptance, defusion, present-moment awareness, self-as-context, values, and committed action.
2. **DBT:** Combines validation and change for severe emotion dysregulation and recurrent self-harm. Comprehensive programmes include individual therapy, skills training, coaching, and therapist consultation.
3. **MBCT:** Integrates mindfulness and cognitive therapy, especially for depressive relapse prevention.
4. **MBSR:** Structured mindfulness practice for stress and health-related distress.
5. **FAP:** Uses clinically relevant interpersonal behaviours in the therapeutic relationship as opportunities for change.
6. **Behavioural activation** is sometimes included in contextual/third-wave groupings, though classification varies.

### Shared principles

Focus on function and context; reduce experiential avoidance; develop mindfulness; clarify values; promote meaningful action; use behavioural practice and a collaborative therapeutic relationship.

### Evidence/applications

DBT has evidence for borderline personality disorder and recurrent self-harm in suitable settings. MBCT can reduce depressive relapse risk in selected patients. ACT has been studied in anxiety, depression, chronic pain, and health problems. Evidence varies by condition, comparator, and treatment fidelity.

### Limitations

The category is heterogeneous. Proposed mechanisms overlap, and studies do not always show that acceptance or mindfulness is the unique active ingredient. Some trials are small or use weak comparators. Training and fidelity matter, particularly for comprehensive DBT. Therapies are not interchangeable.

### Conclusion

Third-wave therapies extend CBT by addressing acceptance, mindfulness, context, values, and emotional regulation. Select according to formulation, evidence, preference, and service capacity.
