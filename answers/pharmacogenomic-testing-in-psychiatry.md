## Exam answer

**Introduction [1].**
- **CYP function is under genetic control.** It is inherited and often, but not always, linked to ethnicity.
- **Phenotypes:** **poor, intermediate, normal, and rapid or ultrarapid metabolisers**.
- Pharmacogenomic (PGx) testing uses an individual's variants to predict:
  1. **drug levels and dose** (pharmacokinetics);
  2. **response**;
  3. **serious adverse reactions**.

### 1. Pharmacokinetic genotyping (CYP enzymes) [1]
- **Ancestry differences matter**. For example:
  - **CYP1A2 ultrarapid metabolisers** are commonest in **African Americans**, so expect more clozapine failure or low plasma levels;
  - **CYP2D6 ultrarapid metabolisers**: 7–15% (European) and 10–15% (African) vs 1–5% in other groups;
  - CYP2D6 poor metabolisers: about 1–4% across groups.
- **Clozapine:**
  - metabolised mainly by **CYP1A2**, less by CYP3A4/5; **CYP2D6 plays almost no role**;
  - PM, IM or EM status from CYP1A2, CYP3A4 and CYP3A5 genotypes allows an estimate of the target dose. Genetic analysis is **"the most accurate method of predicting clozapine dose"**;
  - CYP3A5 PM status raises levels;
  - non-CYP variants (NFIB rs28379954; rs2472297) also predict levels;
  - genotyping can **explain low levels** and guide the choice of an enzyme inhibitor.
- **Atomoxetine:** metabolised by **CYP2D6**, with genotype-related variability, so **dose adjustment** may be needed (a CPIC guideline exists).

### 2. Response prediction [1]
- **Clozapine:** three variants reliably predict response:
  - **HTR2A rs6313**: CC 54% vs CT/TT 62%;
  - **HTR2A rs6314**: C 56% vs T 43%;
  - **HTR3A rs1062613**: C 63% vs T 72%.
- Effects are small. **Clinical utility is limited unless variants are combined mathematically** into an overall probability.

### 3. Adverse-reaction prediction (the strongest current use) [1]
- **Carbamazepine:**
  - **HLA-B\*15:02**: sensitivity about **70%**, specificity close to **100%** in certain populations, for serious exfoliative skin reactions;
  - **testing is recommended before prescribing in people from South-East Asia**.
- **Clozapine agranulocytosis:**
  - **HLA-DQB1 6672G>C**: 1,175% higher risk; sensitivity 21.5%, specificity 98.4%, **PPV 5.1%, NPV 99.7%**;
  - **HLA-B\*59:01**: highly predictive but rare (East Asian); PPV 6.4%, NPV 99.3%;
  - HLA-DQB1 126Q and HLA-B 158T; **HLA-DRB1\*04:02** (sixfold risk).
- **Benign ethnic neutropenia:** the **ACKR1 rs2814778 CC genotype (Duffy-null)** causes it. **All patients starting clozapine should be tested** for BEN.
- **Myocarditis and weight gain:** associations are too weak for clinical use.

### Interpreting tests [1]
- Most variants have **low sensitivity but high specificity**.
- Combine with population incidence to get **PPV and NPV**:
  - **a positive result** should prompt **caution and more frequent monitoring**, not necessarily avoidance (PPV is only about 5–6%);
  - **a negative result** reassures when **NPV exceeds 99.6%**, the background agranulocytosis risk being about 0.4%.

### Strengths and limitations (from the sources)
| Strength | Limitation |
|---|---|
| Explains unexpected drug levels (clozapine) | Small effect sizes for response |
| Prevents severe reactions (HLA-B\*15:02 with carbamazepine) | Low sensitivity; low PPV |
| Guides dosing by metaboliser status (clozapine, atomoxetine) | Ethnicity-specific variants; phenotype not always predicted by ancestry |
| BEN testing avoids unnecessary clozapine stopping | Many associations not yet clinically applicable |

**Conclusion.** In current practice, pharmacogenomic testing is most useful for:
- **safety screening** (HLA-B\*15:02 before carbamazepine in South-East Asians; Duffy-null/BEN before clozapine);
- **explaining or predicting drug levels** (CYP1A2/3A4/3A5 for clozapine; CYP2D6 for atomoxetine).

Response prediction remains weak. Results must be read through PPV and NPV in the context of the patient's ancestry.

## In depth

### Clozapine genetics at a glance [1]
| Domain | Variant | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Response | HTR2A rs6313, rs6314; HTR3A rs1062613 | Combined probability modelling |
| Agranulocytosis | HLA-DQB1 6672G>C; HLA-B\*59:01; DRB1\*04:02 | Caution or reassurance (NPV) |
| Neutropenia (benign) | ACKR1 rs2814778 (Duffy-null) | Test everyone before clozapine |
| Levels | CYP1A2, CYP3A4/5; NFIB | Predict target dose; explain low levels |
