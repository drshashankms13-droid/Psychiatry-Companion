## Exam answer

**Case summary.** A 65-year-old retired man.
- **Cognition and function:** misplacing objects; **poor sleep, appetite and self-care**.
- **One episode of wandering**, brought back by neighbours.
- **Vascular risk:** **diabetes and hypertension for more than 20 years**; **mild stroke 2 years ago**; **regular smoking**.
- **Occasional alcohol.**

### 1. Diagnosis
**Most likely: vascular dementia / vascular cognitive impairment (post-stroke)** [1, 2, 3]:
- **multiple vascular risk factors** plus a previous stroke;
- vascular dementia causes about **15%** of dementia [3], and up to about **30% in India** because of the burden of hypertension, diabetes and stroke [1];
- **NINDS-AIREN** probable VaD requires [1]:
  - dementia;
  - cerebrovascular disease (focal signs plus imaging);
  - a **temporal relationship** (onset within 3 months of stroke, or abrupt or stepwise course).
- Check the timing against his stroke 2 years ago. **Mixed AD plus VaD** is very common [1, 2].

**Differentials:**
- **Depression / vascular depression:** poor sleep and appetite and self-neglect may be depressive. The vascular depression ("depression–executive dysfunction") syndrome shows **apathy and psychomotor slowing** more than sadness [4].
- **Alzheimer's disease** (misplacing objects; wandering).
- **Alcohol-related cognitive impairment** (Wernicke–Korsakoff) [1].
- **Reversible causes:** hypoglycaemia, B12 deficiency, hypothyroidism, drugs [1].

### 2. Assessment [1, 2]
- **History:**
  - onset and **course: stepwise vs gradual**;
  - relation to the stroke;
  - **nocturnal confusion**, fluctuation;
  - **personality preservation** (often well preserved in multi-infarct dementia);
  - **insight**, which may be retained and can cause anxiety or depression [2];
  - **emotional lability or emotional incontinence**;
  - quantify alcohol and smoking.
- **Hachinski Ischaemic Score** [1, 2]:
  - abrupt onset 2; stepwise 1; fluctuating 2; nocturnal confusion 1; preserved personality 1; depression 1; somatic complaints 1; emotional incontinence 1; **hypertension 1**; **stroke 2**; atherosclerosis 1; focal symptoms 2; focal signs 2;
  - **7 or more suggests VaD**; **4 or less suggests AD**.
- **Neurological examination:** unequal reflexes, extensor plantars, pseudobulbar signs, gait (marche à petit pas) [2].
- **Cognition:** executive, attention and processing speed; retrieval-type memory deficit [1].
- **Imaging (MRI):** multiple infarcts of different ages, **white-matter hyperintensities** (Fazekas grade), lacunes in the basal ganglia, thalamus and pons [1].
- **Bloods:** glucose, HbA1c, lipids, TSH, B12, folate, LFT (alcohol).

### 3. Management [1, 3]
- **Secondary prevention:**
  - BP **under 130/80**; **statin**; **HbA1c control**; **antiplatelet** therapy (aspirin or clopidogrel);
  - **stop smoking**; reduce or stop alcohol.
- **Symptomatic:** cholinesterase inhibitors (modest evidence; donepezil approved in some countries) and memantine (some evidence). **There is no licensed or established disease-specific treatment for VaD** [1, 3].
- **Depression:** treat it. Executive-dysfunction depression may respond poorly to SSRIs; **problem-solving therapy** helps [4].
- **Sleep and appetite:** treat depression; review drugs.
- **Safety:** wandering (supervision, ID); medication supervision; self-care support.
- **Caregiver education** and support.

**Conclusion.** Long-standing hypertension and diabetes, smoking and a past stroke make **vascular (or mixed) dementia** the leading diagnosis. Plan:
- confirm with history, Hachinski score and MRI;
- exclude depression, alcohol effects and reversible causes;
- focus management on **aggressive vascular secondary prevention** and supportive dementia care.

## In depth

### Subtypes of vascular dementia [1]
| Type | Key features |
|---|---|
| Multi-infarct | Stepwise; focal signs |
| Strategic infarct | Sudden; angular gyrus, thalamus, basal forebrain |
| Subcortical (small vessel) | Gait, urinary symptoms, executive deficits |
| Binswanger's | Extensive periventricular white matter; abulia |
| CADASIL | NOTCH3; migraine, strokes, dementia |
| Mixed (AD plus VaD) | Very common in the elderly |
