## Exam answer

### Introduction

Online psychotherapy is the delivery of psychological interventions through digital communication technologies, including video, telephone, messaging and structured internet-based programmes.

### Modalities

-   Synchronous video or telephone therapy.
-   Asynchronous text or messaging.
-   Guided internet-based CBT.
-   Digital self-help programmes.
-   Hybrid care combining in-person and online sessions.

### Therapies delivered

CBT, behavioural activation, exposure and response prevention, interpersonal therapy, mindfulness-based interventions and selected DBT-informed approaches can be delivered online when clinically appropriate.

### Advantages

1.  Improves access for remote and underserved populations.
2.  Reduces travel time and cost.
3.  Offers flexibility and continuity of care.
4.  May reduce stigma and increase privacy for some patients.
5.  Facilitates follow-up and between-session materials.
6.  Can improve scalability of evidence-based interventions.

### Pitfalls

-   Digital divide, poor connectivity and limited digital literacy.
-   Reduced access to non-verbal cues in some formats.
-   Privacy and confidentiality risks.
-   Difficulty managing emergencies remotely.
-   Challenges in establishing therapeutic boundaries and alliance.
-   Unsuitability for some severe, unstable or high-risk presentations.
-   Risk of poor-quality or unregulated digital services.

### Precautions

Verify identity and location at the beginning of care; obtain informed consent; explain privacy limitations; establish an emergency plan and local contacts; use secure platforms; document care; maintain professional boundaries; and arrange in-person assessment when required.

### Indian context

Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020, the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 and applicable professional standards are relevant. Tele-MANAS provides a national tele-mental health support pathway, though it is not equivalent to a full course of psychotherapy.

### Conclusion

Online psychotherapy can expand access to effective psychological care, but requires careful patient selection, privacy safeguards, emergency planning and adherence to professional standards.
