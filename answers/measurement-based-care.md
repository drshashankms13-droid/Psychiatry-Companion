## Exam answer

### Definition

Measurement-Based Care (MBC) is a systematic approach in which validated measures of symptoms, functioning and treatment outcomes are repeatedly used to inform clinical decisions. It combines standardised measurement with feedback, clinical interpretation and treatment adjustment.

### Objectives

-   Establish baseline severity.
-   Monitor symptom trajectory and treatment response.
-   Detect non-response, deterioration and relapse early.
-   Guide treatment optimisation.
-   Improve shared decision-making and patient engagement.
-   Evaluate functioning, quality of life and service outcomes.

### Components

1.  **Baseline assessment:** symptoms, functioning, comorbidity, risk
    and patient goals.
2.  **Repeated measurement:** use the same validated instrument at
    planned intervals.
3.  **Feedback:** discuss scores and changes with the patient.
4.  **Clinical interpretation:** consider response, non-response,
    adverse effects and contextual factors.
5.  **Treatment adjustment:** continue, optimise, augment or switch
    treatment as appropriate.
6.  **Follow-up:** repeat measurement and revise the plan.

### Common instruments

| Domain | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Depression | PHQ-9, HAM-D, MADRS |
| Anxiety | GAD-7, HAM-A |
| Bipolar disorder | YMRS, MADRS |
| Schizophrenia | PANSS, BPRS |
| OCD | Y-BOCS |
| PTSD | PCL-5 |
| Alcohol use | AUDIT, AUDIT-C |
| ADHD | ASRS |
| Functioning | WHODAS 2.0, Sheehan Disability Scale |
| Quality of life | WHOQOL-BREF |

Measures may be patient-reported, clinician-rated, functional or adverse-effect scales. Select instruments based on validity, reliability, sensitivity to change, language and cultural appropriateness.

### Clinical applications

In depression, PHQ-9 or MADRS can monitor response and prompt review of adherence, diagnosis and treatment adequacy. In bipolar disorder, YMRS and depression measures can track polarity-specific symptoms. PANSS or BPRS can monitor schizophrenia symptoms, while functional measures assess recovery beyond symptom reduction. Y-BOCS can track OCD severity during ERP or pharmacotherapy.

### Advantages

-   Supports objective and consistent monitoring.
-   Identifies non-response earlier.
-   Improves communication and shared decisions.
-   Supports personalised treatment.
-   Facilitates audit and quality improvement.
-   Tracks functional outcomes as well as symptoms.

### Limitations

-   Adds time and documentation burden.
-   Scores may not capture cultural context or individual priorities.
-   Over-reliance on numbers can replace nuanced clinical judgement.
-   Literacy, language and digital access may limit use.
-   Clinicians may collect scores without acting on them.
-   Measurement cannot compensate for lack of treatment availability.

### Indian context

Use culturally and linguistically validated instruments, integrate brief measures into primary care and telepsychiatry, train clinicians and protect digital data. Tele-MANAS and other digital pathways may support continuity, but scales must be interpreted within the patient's social and clinical context.

### Conclusion

MBC strengthens psychiatric care by combining repeated standardised assessment with feedback and treatment decisions. It does not replace clinical judgement; it makes clinical decision-making more systematic and transparent.
