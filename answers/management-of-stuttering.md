## Exam answer

### Definition

Stuttering (childhood-onset fluency disorder) is a neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by repetitions of sounds or syllables, prolongations, blocks, circumlocutions, and speech produced with excessive tension. Secondary features include avoidance, anxiety, eye blinking, and negative beliefs about communication. Distinguish persistent stuttering from normal developmental disfluency.

### Assessment

Establish age at onset, duration, family history, developmental and language history, frequency and type of disfluency, secondary behaviours, avoidance, distress, school and social impact, and co-occurring conditions. A speech-language pathologist assesses speech fluency and communication participation. Obtain caregiver and teacher reports where appropriate; assess bullying and anxiety.

### Management

- **Preschool children:** Early referral is appropriate when stuttering persists, worsens, causes distress, or there is a family history. Evidence-based options include the Lidcombe Program (parent-delivered behavioural treatment with clinician coaching) and Palin Parent–Child Interaction therapy. Some mild recent disfluency may be monitored with planned review.
- **School-age children:** Combine speech strategies (e.g., gentle onset, pausing, rate regulation) with work on confidence, participation, and reactions to stuttering.
- **Adolescents/adults:** Speech restructuring, stuttering modification, desensitization, CBT for anxiety/avoidance, and communication-participation goals may be used.
- **Family/school:** Provide psychoeducation, prevent bullying, allow time to speak, listen attentively, and avoid finishing sentences or repeatedly instructing the child to "slow down."
- **Medication:** No medication has established routine efficacy for core developmental stuttering. Treat comorbid disorders separately.

### Prognosis

Many preschool children recover. Persistence is more likely with longer duration, later onset, family history, and co-occurring speech-language difficulties. Treatment should prioritize communication and quality of life, not only zero disfluency.
