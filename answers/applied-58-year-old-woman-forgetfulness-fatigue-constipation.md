## Exam answer

**Case summary.** A 58-year-old woman with **diabetes and hypertension** on regular treatment.
- **6 months** of **forgetfulness, easy fatigue, sleep difficulty and constipation**.
- **Unable to do routine housework.**
- **Took voluntary retirement** from her bank job a year ago.

### 1. Differential diagnosis
1. **Hypothyroidism: reversible cognitive impairment** [1]:
   - the fatigue–constipation–forgetfulness cluster;
   - hypothyroidism causes **cognitive slowing, memory impairment, depression**, even psychosis;
   - check **TSH and free T4**;
   - levothyroxine usually improves cognition within **6–12 weeks**.
2. **Depressive episode with cognitive complaints (depressive pseudodementia)** [1, 2, 3]:
   - insomnia, fatigue, functional decline and a **role transition** (voluntary retirement);
   - clues: onset linked to mood, prominent subjective complaints, **"I don't know"** answers, inconsistent performance;
   - **hypothyroidism is a classic reversible cause of depressive symptoms** [3].
3. **Vascular cognitive impairment / early vascular dementia** [1, 4]:
   - **diabetes and hypertension** are vascular risk factors;
   - diabetes raises the risk of cognitive decline **1.2–1.5-fold**;
   - **depression plus diabetes roughly doubles dementia risk** [4];
   - India carries a disproportionately high vascular dementia burden [1].
4. **Mild cognitive impairment or early Alzheimer's disease:** less likely at 58 with these systemic features, but keep it in mind [1].
5. **Other reversible causes ("DEMENTIAS")** [1]:
   - **drugs** (antihypertensives, sedatives);
   - **metabolic**: **B12/folate** deficiency, renal or hepatic failure, **hypo- or hyperglycaemia** (relevant in diabetes);
   - eye and ear;
   - infections;
   - space-occupying lesion.

### 2. Assessment [1, 2]
- **History:**
  - onset and course;
  - **mood** (anhedonia, hopelessness, guilt, suicidal ideas);
  - sleep pattern;
  - the reason for retirement (cognitive or mood difficulty at work?);
  - informant account;
  - **cold intolerance, weight gain, hoarse voice, dry skin** (thyroid);
  - glycaemic control and **hypoglycaemic episodes**;
  - medications; alcohol.
- **Mental state:** depressive cognitions, effort, and the **consistency** of cognitive performance.
- **Examination:** general appearance ("myxoedema or malnutrition"), BP, focal neurological signs [2].
- **Cognitive testing:** **MMSE, MoCA** (better for executive and early impairment), ACE-III. Severity by **CDR** [1].
- **Investigations:**
  - FBC, urea, electrolytes, LFT (minimum screen) [2];
  - **TSH, free T4, B12, folate**, glucose and **HbA1c**, lipids, calcium;
  - VDRL and HIV if indicated;
  - **CT or MRI brain** (infarcts, white matter hyperintensities) [1].

### 3. Management
- **Hypothyroidism, if confirmed:** **levothyroxine**. Reassess cognition and mood after 6–12 weeks [1].
- **Depression, if present:** antidepressant treatment. Pseudodementia improves with it [1, 3].
  - **Problem-solving therapy** if executive dysfunction (vascular depression) is prominent [3].
  - Follow up for later dementia [2].
- **Vascular risk control** [1]:
  - BP **under 130/80**, HbA1c control, statin, smoking cessation;
  - antiplatelets for secondary prevention if cerebrovascular disease is shown.
- **Correct B12 or folate** deficiency if present [1].
- **Psychosocial:** address the **loss of the work role** after voluntary retirement; activity scheduling; family support.
- **Review** cognition over time. Lack of clear confirmation means regular review [2].

**Conclusion.** The leading differentials are:
- **hypothyroidism** (reversible);
- **depression with cognitive impairment**;
- **vascular cognitive impairment** on a diabetic and hypertensive background.

Work-up should exclude reversible causes, treat the cause found, control vascular risk and follow her longitudinally.

## In depth

### Distinguishing depression from dementia (NB-16 table) [1]
| Feature | Pseudodementia | Dementia |
|---|---|---|
| Onset | Related to a depressive episode | Insidious |
| Complaints | Prominent | Minimised |
| Answers | "I don't know" | Near-misses, confabulation |
| Antidepressant | Improves cognition | No improvement |
