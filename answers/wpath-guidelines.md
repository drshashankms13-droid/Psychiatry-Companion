## Exam answer

### Introduction

The World Professional Association for Transgender Health (WPATH) is an international multidisciplinary organisation that develops standards of care for transgender and gender-diverse people. Standards of Care Version 8 (SOC8), published in 2022, supersedes Version 7.

### Principles

-   Respect for gender diversity and human rights.
-   Patient-centred, individualised care.
-   Informed consent and shared decision-making.
-   Avoidance of stigma and unnecessary pathologisation.
-   Cultural humility and attention to social determinants.
-   Access to evidence-informed care across the lifespan.

### Scope of SOC8

SOC8 includes guidance on care for children, adolescents and adults; assessment; hormone therapy; surgery; primary care; reproductive health; voice and communication; mental health; and institutional environments.

### Assessment and mental health

Assessment should explore goals, health needs, capacity, expectations and relevant psychosocial context. Mental health professionals may provide support and assess conditions that affect treatment planning, but should not impose stereotyped requirements. A psychiatric diagnosis is not a prerequisite for being transgender.

### Clinical relevance

SOC8 promotes individualised pathways rather than a single mandatory sequence of social transition, hormones and surgery. Care should be collaborative and consider fertility, sexual health, family context and postoperative needs.

### Indian context and limitations

International standards require adaptation to local law, culture, service availability and resources. The NALSA judgment and Transgender Persons Act 2019 are relevant to Indian practice. Evidence quality varies across interventions and age groups, so clinicians should communicate uncertainty transparently.

### Conclusion

WPATH SOC8 provides a comprehensive, affirming framework for gender-affirming healthcare. Its central message is respectful, individualised care grounded in autonomy and evidence.
