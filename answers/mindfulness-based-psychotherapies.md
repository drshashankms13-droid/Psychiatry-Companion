## Exam answer

### Definition

Mindfulness is intentional attention to present-moment experience with openness and reduced automatic judgment. Mindfulness-based therapies change the person's relationship to thoughts, emotions, bodily sensations, and urges; they are not simply relaxation or thought suppression.

### Major approaches

- **MBSR:** Group programme using body scan, sitting meditation, mindful movement, and home practice for stress and illness-related coping.
- **MBCT:** Combines mindfulness and cognitive therapy, particularly to prevent depressive relapse in recurrent depression.
- **DBT:** Uses mindfulness as a core skill alongside distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness.
- **ACT:** Uses present-moment awareness, acceptance, and defusion in the service of values-based action.

### Proposed mechanisms

Increased awareness of mood shifts, reduced rumination and experiential avoidance, attentional regulation, emotional regulation, and decentring (seeing thoughts as mental events rather than facts). Mechanisms are plausible but not fully established.

### Evidence

MBCT has evidence for reducing depressive relapse risk in selected people with recurrent depression. Mindfulness programmes can improve stress and some anxiety symptoms and may help selected patients with chronic pain or emotional dysregulation. They are not universal treatments or replacements for established care in severe depression, mania, psychosis, or acute suicidality.

### Delivery and precautions

Explain rationale and expectations; provide guided practice and home exercises; adapt for disability, trauma, culture, and comfort. Some participants experience anxiety, traumatic memories, depersonalization, or worsening distress. Offer choice, grounding, eyes-open or movement alternatives, and stop or modify practice if destabilizing. Intensive practice in active mania, severe psychosis, or acute risk requires stabilization and supervision.

### Conclusion

Mindfulness-based therapies are structured, evidence-informed options. Appropriate selection, trained delivery, adaptation, and monitoring are essential.
