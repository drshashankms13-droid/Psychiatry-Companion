## Exam answer

### Introduction

OCD is characterised by obsessions and/or compulsions and is associated with dysfunction in cortico-striato-thalamo-cortical (CSTC) circuits, neurotransmitter systems and cognitive-control networks.

### CSTC circuitry

Key structures include:
- Orbitofrontal cortex (OFC).
- Anterior cingulate cortex (ACC).
- Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex.
- Striatum, including caudate and putamen.
- Globus pallidus and thalamus.

Abnormal activity and connectivity within these circuits may contribute to intrusive thoughts, error monitoring, threat appraisal and repetitive behaviour.

### Direct and indirect pathways

The traditional model proposes imbalance between direct and indirect basal ganglia pathways, resulting in excessive propagation of CSTC activity. Current models are more complex and include multiple loops and network interactions.

### Neurotransmitters

-   **Serotonin:** response to serotonin reuptake inhibitors supports
    involvement, though the mechanism is not fully understood.
-   **Dopamine:** implicated in habit formation, reward and tic-related
    symptoms.
-   **Glutamate:** glutamatergic abnormalities have motivated research
    into glutamate-modulating treatments.
-   **GABA:** inhibitory signalling may contribute to circuit
    regulation.

### Other mechanisms

Functional imaging, structural imaging and neuropsychological studies suggest abnormalities in error monitoring, cognitive flexibility, habit learning and inhibitory control. Neurodevelopmental and immune mechanisms may be relevant in subgroups.

### Treatment implications

SSRIs and clomipramine are established pharmacological treatments. Exposure and response prevention (ERP) targets learned compulsive responses. Antipsychotic augmentation may help selected treatment-resistant cases. Deep brain stimulation and other neuromodulation approaches are reserved for carefully selected severe refractory OCD.

### Limitations

Neuroimaging findings are group-level and heterogeneous. No single circuit abnormality is diagnostic or sufficient to explain all clinical presentations.

### Conclusion

OCD is best understood as a disorder involving interacting CSTC circuits, neurotransmitter systems and cognitive-behavioural mechanisms. These models inform pharmacological, psychological and neuromodulation treatments.
